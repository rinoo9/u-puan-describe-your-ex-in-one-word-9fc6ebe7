Entry for the make.fun bounty "Describe Your Ex in One Word": the entry word is "LeftOnRead" (10 characters).

## Why it works

"LeftOnRead" compresses the whole relationship into one word. They read the message, they saw the message, and they chose silence as their reply. No breakup speech, no closure, just two blue ticks and eternal nothing. It is not what they said. It is what they did not.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`. Two runs produce byte-identical output ("LeftOnRead", verified 26 Nov 2026).
